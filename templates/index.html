Architecture & Machine Learning Methodology

Understanding the data pipeline, feature engineering, and inference flow.

Step 1

Text Preprocessing & Normalization

Raw text passes through an extensive cleaning pipeline: HTML tags, URLs, email addresses, and non-alphanumeric noise are stripped. Standard English contractions (e.g., don't → do not) are expanded, and functional stopwords are selectively filtered while retaining context.

Step 2

TF-IDF N-Gram Vectorization

We extract Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) features with unigrams and bigrams (`ngram_range=(1, 2)`). Bigrams enable capturing contextual phrases like "peer-reviewed study" or "secret miracle cure" that individual words miss.

Step 3

Stylometric & Heuristic Signals

Fake and sensational news often relies on distinct psychological levers: excessive uppercase letters (all-caps shouting), high exclamation mark frequency, and clickbait words. Our system extracts these quantitative stylometric indicators in tandem with statistical NLP features.

Step 4

Passive-Aggressive & Ensemble Modeling

The core classifier employs the Passive-Aggressive Classifier, an online margin-based algorithm renowned for high precision on sparse text classification tasks. We calibrate its margin distance into smooth probabilities using Sigmoid calibration (`CalibratedClassifierCV`), benchmarked against Naive Bayes and Logistic Regression.
